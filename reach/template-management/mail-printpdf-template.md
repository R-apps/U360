# Mail PrintPDF Template

The Mail Print PDF templates are standardized print-ready formats often used for printable letters, bills, or statements that can be mailed to recipients, or can be converted to print as physical copies.

### Create a New Mail Print Template

To create a template:

* Go to **Template Management** > **Print Template** > **Create Template**. A visual HTML editor will open to design your template.
*   Using the elements in the editor, create a template catering to your requirements. All the elements are available with placeholders for dynamic content.<br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (19) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   Once done, click <> in the editor. Give your template a name, tag name, and category. Select the permissions and then click **Save**.<br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The template gets created and is available under “View Templates”.

### View Mail Print Templates

All the Mail Print templates you created will be available here.

To view templates, go to **Template Management** > **SMS Text Template** > **View Template**

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* To preview a template, hover on it and click **View**.
* To edit the template, hover on it and click **Edit**.
* To search for a template, you can choose to search by name, tag, or category. Type in the search box and click **Search**.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* If you do not require a template, hover on it and click **Delete**.
