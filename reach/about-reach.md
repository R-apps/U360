# About Reach

## What's Reach?

**Reach** is a unified omnichannel communication and campaign automation platform that helps businesses deliver personalized, multi-channel messages at scale. Whether you're just starting out or running enterprise-level campaigns, Reach helps you talk to your customers in the right place, at the right time and scale your outreach effortlessly. Reach makes it easy to connect with your audience across **SMS, Email, WhatsApp, Social, IVR, App Notifications**, and more.

Reach allows enterprises right from designing the communication templates to sending them to target audience - all from one place. This centralized communication hub supports end-to-end communications, eliminating the vendor dependencies.

The Administrator (Super Admin) governs the platform based on the Role-Based-Access-Control.&#x20;

### Why Use Reach?

Marketers, customer service teams, and business operations, who seek results with reduced complexity. Whether you are sending alerts, running promotions, or nurturing leads, Reach is your go-to platform for reaching faster, smarter, and at scale.

Reach helps you create consistent messaging across platforms for a seamless and immersive brand experience.
