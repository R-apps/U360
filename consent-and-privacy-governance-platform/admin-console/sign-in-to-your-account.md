# Sign in to Your Account

Every business is considered a client. We, at Intense, create a client granting all the Administrator permissions for the business Administrator(s) to customize their customer portal with required categories and channels to capture, enforce, and manage their customers consent and data privacy.

Use the credentials shared by Intense team to log in to the Admin Portal.

Enter the URL: [https://platform.intense.in:9071/consent-management-system/admin/sign-in](https://platform.intense.in:9071/consent-management-system/admin/sign-in) and sign in with the provided credentials.

The platform opens the [Dashboard](../), where you can configure consent models, communication channels, widgets, customer journeys, branding, compliance settings, and other portal experiences presented to customers.
