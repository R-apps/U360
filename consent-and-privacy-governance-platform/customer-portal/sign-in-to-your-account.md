# Signing into your account

To sign in to your account,&#x20;

* Open a browser and enter the URL:\
  [https://platform.intense.in:9071/consent-management-system/customer/sign-in](https://platform.intense.in:9071/consent-management-system/customer/sign-in) .
*   Enter your registered mobile number and select **Send OTP**.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Login Page.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   Enter the OTP received to your mobile number and select **Verify OTP**.<br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Login Page with OTP.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

After signing in, the platform opens the [Privacy Preference Center](privacy-preference-center.md), where you can view and manage your consent preferences and data privacy settings.
