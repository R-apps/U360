# Customer Complaints

The **Customer Complaints** section allows you to review, track, and manage customer feedback and support requests related to consent and data preferences.

For every customer complaint, the system records every detail of it - customer id, category, complaint raised date and time, status, and the actions to update after addressing the complaint.

Refresh it periodically to reload the latest complaint records.

You can even use search to find complaints by category.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Periodically mark the complaint's status here to update the customer about the progress of their complaint. To update,

* Select **Update**
* Select the Update Status (Submitted / In Progress / Resolved / Closed)
* Keep a note of the status (E.g., If resolved, add resolution notes)
* Draft a message to update the customer about their compaint status.

Then, select **Save Changes**.
