# File Complaints

For any concerns pertaining to consent or data authorization preferences, file a complaint.

To file, click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png>)  and

* Select the category
* Date on when you encountered it
* Describe your concern in detail
* Attach a file (size < 10MB) in supported formats&#x20;
* Select the preferred resolution and contact method

Then, click **Submit Complaint**.&#x20;

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1).png" alt="" width="285"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

After submitting a complaint, you can track its status in "My Complaints" page.

To view your complaints, click **My Complaints** (<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2) (1).png" alt="" data-size="original">).
