# Privacy Center

The **Privacy Center** gives you direct control over your personal data and consent, with clear visibility into all your requests.

Use the Privacy Center when you want to:

* Exercise your data rights
* Correct or remove personal information
* Keep a record of your consent
* Report any privacy-related issue

You can raise requests for:

* **Consent Certificate**: Download an official record of your consent as proof of your consent preferences.
* **Data Correction**: Submit a request to update or fix incorrect personal information.
* **Data Deletion**: Request removal of your personal data from the system, as per applicable policies.
* **Privacy Complaint**: Report any concern related to consent usage or data handling.

All your requests are listed under **Your Privacy Requests** with status and unique request id. \
You can monitor progress and track updates of your privacy requests.
