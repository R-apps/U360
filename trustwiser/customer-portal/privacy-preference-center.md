# Privacy Preference Center

The **Privacy Preference Center** is a self-service interface where you can view, manage, and control your consent and data preferences across services and communication channels.

This provides a complete view of personal details, consent status, and communication settings; giving you full control and visibility over your consent, ensuring that communication aligns with your preferences.

This page lets you decide what you want to receive, how you want to receive it, and gives you full visibility into how your consent is applied.

This portal ensures that:

* You stay in control of your communication preferences
* Your consent is respected across all services
* Your data usage remains transparent and compliant

{% hint style="info" %}
The dashboard you see here is a sample view. Since consent preferences differ across business verticals and industry use cases, the options may vary based on how each business configures it to meet its specific requirements.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Privacy Preference Center.png" alt="Privacy Preference Center"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Each section in this dashboard is referred to as a widget. Let's walk through each widget.

#### **Personal Data Management**

This section displays your personal data such as name, email, mobile number, and other identifiers. Review this information to ensure accuracy.&#x20;

If the business permits you to make changes (Edit, Delete) to your personal information, you can make necessary corrections or even delete it, else, you can only view.&#x20;

**Example**: To change or correct your Full Name, click <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Edit icon (1).png" alt="Edit Field Icon" data-size="line"> , enter the new name, attach a supporting document, if necessary. Then, select **Confirm Update** to submit the request.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Update Personal Information.png" alt="Update Personal Data" width="504"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

After the request is processed, you get notified and the field gets updated.

{% hint style="info" %}
To delete you personal data, select **Request Data Deletion** and raise a deletion request. \
<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Data Deletion Request.png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

#### Consent Preferences

Here is where you manage your consent preferences for different services the business offers.&#x20;

This widget lists the services (loan offers, insurance products, and other pertaining to the business) for which the business requires consent. Each service shows its current statusve or Revoked)

* Select **Grant** to allow communication for a service
* Select **Revoke** to withdraw consent and stop further communications for that service

In both the cases, the system asks you for the confirmation. Select **Accept** or **Revoke** in the \
**Consent Confirmation** dialog to provide your confirmation.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Consent COnfirmation.png" alt="" width="502"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The system updates your consent status immediately.

#### Manage your Preferred Channels

This widget lists all communication channels the business is currently using to communicate to its users. You can choose to opt-in or opt-out of a communication channel based on your preference.

*   To opt-in, turn ON the Toggle button (![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (145).png>)), and then select "Enable Channel" in the \
    **Channel Confirmation** dialog.<br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Enable Channel.png" alt="" width="510"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   To opt-out, turn OFF the Toggle button (![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (146).png>)), and then select "Disable Channel" in the \
    **Channel Confirmation** dialog.<br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Disable Channel.png" alt="" width="508"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### **Communication Transparency**

This widget shows how your consent is applied in real time. It includes:

* **Messages sent** – Number of communications delivered
* **Blocked by consent** – Messages not sent due to your preferences
* **Suppressed after revocation** – Messages stopped after you withdrew consent
* **External data access** – Any authorized data sharing activity

This gives clear visibility into how your data and permissions are being used.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Communication Transparency.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### Data Protection Information

The widget includes a few FAQs which you can refer to understand the DPDP Act, your rights, data protection, and your consent preferences.

Select **DPDPA FAQs** to view and understand how your data is handled.

#### Get Support

If you encounter any issues with managing your consent preferences, use our support options to get the assistance round the clock.

Select **About your consents** to see the available support options.
