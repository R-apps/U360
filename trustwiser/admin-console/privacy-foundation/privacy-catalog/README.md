# Privacy Catalog

The Privacy Catalog lets administrators configure and manage the data fields, privacy notices, processing purposes, communication channels, sharing partners, systems, and consent categories used across the privacy platform. It provides a central place to define how personal information is presented, why it is processed, how consent preferences are organized, and which channels and systems support privacy-related activities.

By maintaining these configurations in one place, organizations can promote consistent privacy practices, manage customer preferences, and support their data protection and consent management processes.

Configuring an integration documents data-sharing arrangements and governance settings. It does not, by itself, establish DPDP compliance. Organizations must also implement the required technical, organizational, and legal safeguards for their data processing activities.
