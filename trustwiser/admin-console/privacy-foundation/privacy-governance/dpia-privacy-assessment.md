# DPIA/Privacy Assessment

Organizations process personal data across multiple business activities, systems, and third-party integrations. These activities may introduce privacy risks, such as unauthorized access, excessive data collection, inappropriate data sharing, or inadequate data protection measures.

This section lets you assess, and monitor privacy risks associated with their personal data processing activities, sharing partners, and system integrations.

The page provides three assessment categories:

* RoPA
* Sharing Partners
* Systems

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/DPIA - Privacy Assessment.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Assessment methods

Select **Run Assessment** to initiate the privacy assessment for the corresponding processing activity. Select **View** to review the assessment details and its current status.

Privacy owners can complete an assessment using either of the following methods:

* **In-platform assessment**: Run and complete the assessment directly within the platform. This option allows privacy owners to access the questionnaire, provide responses, and submit the assessment without leaving the application.
* **Email-based assessment**: Send the assessment through email to the concerned stakeholders. Recipients can access and complete the assessment using the email link without logging in to the platform.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/DPIA - Privacy Assessment (Run).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

This flexibility allows organizations to involve internal teams and external stakeholders in the assessment process, even if they do not have access to the platform.
