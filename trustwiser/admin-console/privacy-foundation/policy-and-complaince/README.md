# Policy & Complaince

The Policy & Compliance module helps organizations manage privacy policies, map them to personal data processing activities, monitor compliance, track policy exceptions, conduct periodic reviews, and maintain compliance evidence.

It provides a centralized view of privacy governance activities, enabling administrators and privacy teams to define organizational policies, associate them with relevant business processes, evaluate compliance status, and maintain an audit trail.

The module supports organizations in implementing and monitoring privacy requirements under applicable data protection regulations, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.
