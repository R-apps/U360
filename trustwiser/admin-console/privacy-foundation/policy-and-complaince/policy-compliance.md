# Policy Compliance

This provides an overview of how effectively organizational privacy policies are implemented across their mapped processing activities, systems, consent categories, and sharing partners.

It displays an overall Privacy Policy Compliance score and a policy-wise compliance breakdown.&#x20;

It helps

* identify policies with lower compliance scores.
* highlight gaps in policy mapping and implementation.
* privacy teams prioritize policies that require attention.

Use **Refresh** to retrieve the latest compliance information.
