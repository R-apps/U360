# Policy Reviews

The **Policy Reviews** section allows administrators to record the outcome of privacy policy reviews and maintain a history of review activities.

Regular reviews help organizations ensure that their privacy policies remain relevant to current business operations, data processing practices, and regulatory requirements. It

* Establishes accountability for policy reviews.
* Maintains a record of review decisions.
* Helps organizations track upcoming policy reviews.
* Provides historical information for internal audits.

### Recording a Policy Review

To record a policy review,

* Under **Record Review**, select the policy you want to review.
* Enter the Reviewer **Name** responsible for conducting the review.
* Select the **Outcome** of the review (Approved / Needs Changes / Deferred)
* Enter the **Review Date** and specify the **Next Review Date** to indicate when the policy should be reviewed again.
* Enter any relevant observations or findings in the **Comments** field.

Select **Save Review** to record the review.
