# Policy Exceptions

The **Policy Exceptions** section allows administrators to monitor exceptions associated with organizational privacy policies.

A policy exception represents a situation in which a business process, system, or other activity does not fully comply with an established policy or requires an approved deviation.

Policy exceptions help organizations identify and manage situations where established privacy requirements are not fully met.

The platform displays exception records with information such as the policy name, reference ID, review date, and current status.
