# Policy Mapping

The **Policy Mapping** section allows administrators to define where and how a selected privacy policy applies within the organization. A policy may apply to multiple business processes, systems, and third-party relationships. Mapping establishes these relationships and provides visibility into the scope of each policy.

A policy can be associated with relevant RoPA records, consent categories, systems, sharing partners, and personal data fields.

This helps organizations identify which business operations and entities must follow the policy and makes compliance monitoring more structured.

### Mapping a Policy

To map a privacy policy,&#x20;

* Select it from Policy list.
* Under RoPA Processes, select the processing activities to which the policy applies.
* Under Consent Categories, select the relevant consent categories.
* Under Systems/Integrations, select the systems associated with the policy.
* Under Sharing Partners, select the relevant third-party partners.
* Review the associated Personal Data Fields.
* Enter any additional information in the Notes field, if required.
* Select **Save Mapping** to save the configuration.
