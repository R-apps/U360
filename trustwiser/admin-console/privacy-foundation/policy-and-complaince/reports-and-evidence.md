# Reports & Evidence

The Reports & Evidence section helps organizations generate compliance reports and evidence packages using the information maintained in the Policy & Compliance module.

It provides a consolidated view of policy records, mappings, compliance information, exceptions, and reviews. It

* helps prepare supporting documentation for internal audits.
* provides visibility into policy coverage and compliance performance.
* helps management review outstanding exceptions and policy reviews.

Use **Generate Evidence Pack** to generate a consolidated evidence package containing relevant policy and compliance information. The generated report also displays its generation date and the reporting scope.

Use **Download JSON** to download the available report information in JSON format for further processing or integration.&#x20;
