# Policy Repository

The Policy Repository serves as the central location for creating, storing, and managing organizational privacy policies. It allows administrators to maintain policies covering different aspects of data protection, privacy management, and organizational governance.

A centralized policy repository helps organizations maintain consistent privacy standards across departments and business operations. It also makes policies easier to create, locate, update, and review.

E.g., Data Detection of Harmful Status, Third-Party Data Sharing Policy, Customer Privacy Policy, and more.

Each policy is displayed as an individual card containing information such as its name, description, status, version, and associated categories or business areas.

### Creating a Policy

To create a policy:

* Select **Create Policy**.
* Enter the required policy details:
  * Policy information such as Name, Code, Type, Applicability, Description, Owner name, Department, and Approver.
  * Specify the policy's version, validity period, lifecycle status, and change history. These help organizations maintain traceability, monitor policy validity, schedule reviews, and track changes over time.
  * Select the Policy's content type and upload it. You can use **Policy Content** field to manage the policy content within the repository rather than relying on an uploaded document.
  * Select Yes/No accordingly if the policy requires approval and employees acknowlegement.

Then, select **Create Policy**. The system assigns a version to the policy when it is saved. Subsequent updates create new version entries, allowing you to maintain a record of policy changes.&#x20;
