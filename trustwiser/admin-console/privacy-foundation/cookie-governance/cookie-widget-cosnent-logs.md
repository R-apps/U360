# Cookie Widget Cosnent Logs

Use **Cookie Widget Consent Logs** to view and audit visitor consent decisions captured across your published cookie banners. Every interaction—whether a visitor accepts all cookies, rejects non-essential cookies, or customizes their preferences, is recorded to maintain an audit trail and support regulatory compliance.

To access, select the **Cookie Widget Consent Logs** tab in the **Cookie Governance** Page.

The **Cookie Widget Consent Activity** summary displays an overview of recorded consent actions across deployed widgets.

* **Total Activity Count:** Displays the total number of recorded consent actions, including **Accept, Reject,** and **Confirm My Choices**.
* **Accepted:** Shows the total number of acceptance decisions.
* **Rejected:** Shows the total number of rejection decisions.
* **Updated:** Shows the total number of preference updates submitted by visitors.

These metrics help you monitor visitor consent activity and understand acceptance, rejection, and preference update trends.

Use **Search** and **Filter** options to view the activity of a visitor with the necessary columns. This allows you to locate a visitor's consent history, review specific activity, and focus on relevant details without navigating through the entire log history.
