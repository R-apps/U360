# Cookie Policy

A **cookie policy** informs visitors about the cookies and tracking technologies used on your website, their purpose, and how visitors can manage their consent preferences. This helps organizations maintain transparency and support compliance with privacy regulations such as the GDPR.

Generating a policy from the scanned inventory helps ensure that visitors receive information about the cookies and tracking scripts actually used on the website. Use the **Cookie Policy** tab to generate and manage cookie policies for your registered websites.

To access, select the **Cookie Policy** tab.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Cookie Policy.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Generate a Cookie Policy

To generating a policy, follow these steps:

1. **Scan First:** Run a website scan to identify the latest cookies and tracking scripts.
2. **Generate:** Generate the policy using the scanned inventory. The document includes cookie category tables, third-party services, and consent instructions.
3. **Publish:** Copy the public policy URL and link it from your website, such as in the footer. Regenerate the policy after subsequent scans to keep its content up to date.

Configure policy details by providing the following information:

* Enter the policy name (**Policy Title**) to be displayed for the generated cookie policy.
* Specify the URL path (**Publish Path**) where the policy will be accessible.&#x20;
* Select All or a required website (**Website Scope)** for which the policy must be generated.&#x20;

Then, select **Generate Policy** to create the cookie policy using the configured details and scanned inventory.

### Manage Published Policies

The **Published Policies** section displays the generated cookie policies, along with their title, publish path, and generation date and time.

* Use **Refresh** (![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Refresh icon.png>)) to retrieve the latest policy records.
* Switch between **Card** (![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Cards View icon.png>)) and **Table** (![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Table View icon.png>)) layouts to view the policy records accordingly.
*   Use **Preview** to view the generated policy. <br>

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Cookie Policy - Preview.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* **Open Live:** Open the policy at its public URL to verify how it appears to visitors.
* **Delete:** Remove a policy that is no longer required.

The generation timestamp helps identify when a policy was last generated and determine whether it needs to be updated.
