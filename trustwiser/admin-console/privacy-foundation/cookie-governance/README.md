# Cookie Governance

Use **Cookie Governance** to discover, classify, and manage website cookies and tracking scripts through a centralized inventory, consent controls, and audit reporting.

To access, go to **Privacy Foundation** > **Cookie Governance** in the left navigation menu. The module is organized into five core tabs:

* **Website Scanner**: Register website URLs and run automated or manual scans to detect cookies and tracking scripts.
* **Cookie Inventory**: Review, classify, and govern all discovered and unclassified cookies across registered websites.
* **Cookie Consent Widget**: Configure and customize cookie consent banners for your domains.
* **Cookie Widget Consent Logs**: Audit and track user consent records captured through your cookie widgets.
* **Cookie Policy**: Generate and maintain compliant cookie policies aligned with discovered trackers.
