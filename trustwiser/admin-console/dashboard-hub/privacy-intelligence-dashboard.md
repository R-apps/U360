# Privacy Intelligence Dashboard

The **Privacy Intelligence** dashboard provides insights of the organization's consent configuration, communication channels, sharing partners, and system integrations. The dashboard combines key metrics, business insights, visual trends, and AI-assisted recommendations to help administrators assess the current privacy configuration. You can review the status of consent categories and communication channels, understand how personal data is used for different purposes, monitor sharing-partner visibility and default settings, review system integrations, and identify opportunities to strengthen privacy-by-default practices and consent alignment.

Use this dashboard to understand privacy configuration and consent readiness.

It helps administrators understand how privacy and consent settings are configured across the organization and identify areas that may require attention.&#x20;

The dashboard provides insights on the following:

* **Consent categories** – View the total number of consent categories and their active, visible, hidden, or inactive status to understand the availability and visibility of consent options.
* **Communication channels** – View configured communication channels and their status to identify the channels currently available for customer communications.
* **Sharing partners** – View the total number of configured sharing partners and their visibility to customers to understand how data-sharing relationships are presented to customers.
* **System integrations** – View the configured platform and custom integrations to understand the systems connected to the privacy platform.
* **Business Snapshot** – View a consolidated summary of the organization's privacy configuration for the selected period to quickly assess its current privacy posture.
* **Consent Categories** – Compare active and visible categories with hidden or inactive categories to identify consent categories that may require review.
* **Privacy-by-Default** – View consent categories based on their default settings to assess whether privacy-friendly options are enabled by default.
* **Channels** – View the distribution of active and inactive communication channels to identify channels that are currently available or require attention.
* **Purpose Utilization** – View the purposes for which personal data is used to understand how data processing is distributed across business purposes.
* **Partner Visibility** – Compare sharing partners that are visible to customers with those that are hidden to assess transparency in data-sharing practices.
* **Partner Default Setting** – View the default privacy settings configured for sharing partners to assess whether data-sharing preferences follow a privacy-by-default approach.
* **Integrations** – Compare platform and custom integrations to understand the distribution of connected systems and integration types.
* **Opportunity Insights** – View identified areas for improvement across privacy management, consent, partner governance, and communication channels to help prioritize actions.

Use the available filters to focus the dashboard on a specific date range, business unit, department, consent category, processing purpose, or customer segment.

#### AI Assistant

When enabled, the **AI Assistant** provides prioritized recommendations based on the current privacy posture. It highlights notable conditions across areas such as sharing partners, consent categories, and DPDPA alignment and provides suggested actions for consideration.

These insights help administrators move beyond viewing configuration data to identifying areas that may benefit from review or improvement.
