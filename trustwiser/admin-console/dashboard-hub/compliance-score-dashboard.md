# Compliance Score Dashboard

The **Compliance Score** dashboard provides a consolidated view of where the organization currently stands, which areas require attention, and how its compliance posture is changing over time.&#x20;

The Compliance Score dashboard helps administrators:

* Assess compliance posture through a consolidated compliance score and maturity trend.
* Monitor the implementation of privacy controls.
* Identify compliance gaps and prioritize areas that require remediation.
* Evaluate audit readiness across governance, consent, channels, partners, integrations, and operations.
* Compare compliance performance across privacy and operational domains.
* Monitor policy compliance through acknowledgment, pending, and overdue statuses.
* Prioritize improvements using AI-assisted recommendations.

Let's look at each insight:

* **Business Snapshot**—provides a concise summary of the organization's compliance maturity for the selected period, highlighting key conditions such as consent configuration, governance gaps, third-party sharing, and audit readiness.
* **Open Gaps**—This section lists identified compliance gaps by priority. The gaps can include issues related to governance, consent, third-party sharing, and communication channels.
* **Compliance Trend**— This chart shows the organization's maturity score over the selected period.
* **Domain Scorecard**—This compares compliance scores across key privacy and operational domains, including:
  * Governance
  * Consent Management
  * Channels
  * Third-party sharing
  * System integrations
  * Operations and rights
* **Audit Readiness**—This section provides a breakdown of evidence completeness across areas such as:
  * ROPA register
  * DPIA or assessment evidence
  * Consent category approvals
  * Integration approvals
  * Partner visibility controls
  * Retention documentation
* **Department Comparison**—This chart compares business-area maturity across departments or business functions.
* **Policy Compliance**—This section provides an overview of policy acknowledgment and review status, including Acknowledged, Pending, and Overdue items.

Use the filters at the top of the dashboard to focus the displayed information on a specific scope. Select **Reset** to clear the selected filters and return to the default view.
