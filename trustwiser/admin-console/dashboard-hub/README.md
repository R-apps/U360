# Dashboard Hub

The **Privacy Intelligence Dashboards** page serves as a centralized **Dashboard Hub** for accessing dashboards across the platform. It provides a consolidated view of privacy, consent, compliance, risk, governance, and operational insights, helping administrators monitor key aspects of the organization's privacy from a single location. Administrators can select the relevant dashboard to view detailed insights.

### Available dashboards

The dashboard hub provides access to the following dashboards:

<table data-header-hidden><thead><tr><th width="239.5555419921875">Dashboard</th><th>Purpose</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Platform Overview</strong></td><td>Provides an overall view of consent activity, operational metrics, and system health across the platform.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Executive Dashboard</strong></td><td>Provides a high-level view of compliance, privacy risk, consent health, operations, alerts, and administrative actions.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Domain Dashboards</strong></td><td>Provides domain-level views for areas such as risk, compliance, and marketing consent, including detailed insights for further analysis.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Compliance Score</strong></td><td>Provides insights into DPDPA maturity, domain scores, identified gaps, audit readiness, and department-level comparisons.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Privacy Intelligence</strong></td><td>Provides business intelligence based on consent categories, channels, sharing partners, and integrations.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Privacy Risk</strong></td><td>Provides visibility into privacy risks, including risk areas, high-risk assets, pending DPIAs, vendor risks, consent risks, and mitigation progress.</td></tr></tbody></table>

> **Note:** The dashboards available to you may depend on your organization's configuration and access permissions.
