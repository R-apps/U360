# Privacy Requests & Complaints

The **Privacy Requests & Complaints** section helps you manage operational privacy workflows, handle customer data principal rights, investigate complaints, and track incident resolutions across your organization. Maintaining these workflows ensures compliance with DPDP and other global privacy regulations.

To access, go to **Privacy Operations** > **Privacy Requests & Complaints**.

Select either the **Complaints** tab or the **Privacy Requests (DSR)** tab depending on the operational workflow you want to review.

### Customer Complaints

The **Complaints** tab allows your team to track grievances, communications, and resolution activities.

* View real-time counts for Total complaints, Open, In Review, Resolved, Overdue, and SLA Breaches.
* Search to find specific issues, or filter records by _Status_ and _Priority_.
* Review individual tickets containing complaint titles, customer IDs, priority levels, customer and internal statuses, and case ages.
* Select **Export CSV** to export the data.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Privacy Operations - COmplaints (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Use **View** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/View complaint - icon.png>)) to view individual complaint information. Here, you can update the complaint status, change the complaint priority, a resolution note upon resolving the complaint, or assign it to route it to a specific officer or team.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Privacy Operations - COmplaints (Details).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Track Data Subject Requests (DSR)

The **Privacy Requests (DSR)** tab helps you manage requests raised by your personal data access, correction, deletion, and consent withdrawal requests.

You can customize the view by selecting the required columns visibility (such as ID, Customer ID, Type, Field, Priority, Status, SLA Due, and Submission Date), or by filtering the records using the request type.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Privacy Operations - Privacy Requests (DSR).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Use **View** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/View complaint - icon.png>)) tox view individual request details. You can even manage and view the request's history.&#x20;
