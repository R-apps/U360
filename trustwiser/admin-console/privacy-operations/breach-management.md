# Breach Management

Use **Breach Management** to log, investigate, and report data security incidents across your organization. Maintaining a centralized record of breach investigations, remediation actions, and regulatory notifications helps your enterprise meet strict statutory reporting deadlines under global privacy laws.

To access, go to **Privacy Operations** > **Breach Management**. The platform displays the breach activity with the summary cards displaying Total Cases, Open Cases, Under Investigation, Regulator Reported, Overdue Cases, and Closed Cases.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Management.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Investigate and manage a breach case

Select **View** corresponding to the required breach case. The platform opens its dedicated case workspace. The workspace organizes incident response across these six tabs:

*   **Overview**: Update core case details including title, severity, status, assigned owner, breach type, incident type, affected records, discovery and notification timestamps, root cause, affected systems, mitigation actions, and remediation steps. Select **Save Changes** to record your updates

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Overveiw.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   **Investigation**: Document formal investigation findings and record dated investigation notes to track containment and forensic progress.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Investigation.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   **Mitigation**: Add mitigation actions by specifying the action description, owner, target due date, and current status.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Mitigation.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   **Notifications**: Log mandatory disclosures sent to regulatory authorities or affected individuals. Record the notification type, recipient, sent date, delivery method, and confirmation notes.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Notifications.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   **Attachments**: Upload supporting documentation or evidence files with descriptive titles and notes.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Attachments.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   **Audit Trail**: Review the history of every status change, update, and action taken on the case.

    <div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Breach Activity - Audit Trial.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
