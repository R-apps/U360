# Business Objects

The **Business Objects** page lets you define business objects and associate them with data categories for use in data mapping across the platform. A business object represents an entity, such as a customer, employee, vendor, or website visitor. Its associated data categories describe the types of information related to that entity.

Add once and reuse them across the platform.&#x20;

### Adding a Business Object

* Select **+ Add Business Object**&#x20;
* Enter a unique name and a brief description explaining the business object's purpose.
* Under **Data categories**, select **Add category**.
  * Enter the category details and save the category.
  * Repeat these steps to add more categories, as needed.

Repeat these steps to add more categories, as needed.

Then, select **Create**.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Add Business Object.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The new business object appears on the **Business Objects** page. This page displays the business objects as cards with their details— Name, Description, Data categories, Visibility, and Active status.

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Business Objects.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### Adding Business Objects in Bulk

To add multiple business objects at once, select **Download Excel Template** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Download Excel Template icon.png>)) to download the template. Enter the business object details in the specified format and save the file.

Then, select **Import** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Import from Excel.png>)) and upload the excel file.&#x20;

#### Manage Business Objects

* To edit a business object, select **Edit** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Edit icon (1).png>)) to update the business object's name, description, or associated categories.
* Use ACTIVE toggle to enable or disable the business object. Only the active business objects can be used.
* Use **Delete** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Delete Icon.png>)) to delete a business object that is no longer required.
* Use **Refresh** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Refresh icon.png>)) to load all the business objects added to date.
* To export the business objects to an excel file, select **Export** (![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Export to Excel.png>)).
* To reorder the business objects, hover over it and drag it to the required position.
